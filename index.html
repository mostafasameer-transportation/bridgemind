Bridge Intelligence

BridgeMind separates the structural baseline from live operations. The research layer provides risk context; the operational layer shows what is happening now.

01

Structural & Condition Context

Use NBI and inspection attributes such as condition ratings, age, material, span, load posting, and inspection evidence to build a transparent rehabilitation baseline.

02

Network Consequence

Combine traffic importance, alternate routes, connectivity, emergency access, and closure effects so the bridge is evaluated as part of the transportation network.

03

Hazard & Community Context

Bring flood and hurricane exposure together with community vulnerability so rehabilitation and recovery scenarios can account for more than asset condition alone.

Research baseline: The prototype uses the Miami-Dade analysis structure developed for BridgeMind, including a 644-bridge research inventory and 18 Tier 4 critical flags. These are research-layer values, not live agency status. Detailed RNI values and bridge-level rehabilitation history can be connected through the final validated research dataset.
